### 3.6 What is your company going to make?

**AFTER (핵심: 플랫폼 비전, B2B2C)**

Our first 500 users came organically in 5 days from one TikTok video, proving viral demand. We're now building creator tools so anyone can launch their own AI idol and earn from their fanbase.

## 5. Idea

### 5.1 Why did you pick this idea?

**핵심 변경: 개인 스토리 + 플랫폼 인사이트**

I was a K-pop fan who found emotional support through idols during depression. When I built an AI version of my favorite idol, friends asked me to make theirs too. I watched them confide in these AIs about their struggles—that's when I realized: people don't just want to chat with AI, they want to be *fans* of AI.

But here's what I learned next: some friends wanted to *create* AI idols, not just consume them. They had character ideas, backstories, even monetization plans. That's when the real insight hit—**AI companions need creators, not just users.**

YouTube didn't make videos. TikTok didn't make dances. They gave creators tools and audiences. That's what's missing for AI companions: a platform where creators build characters, grow fanbases, and earn money.

We validated this: 500 signups in 5 days from one viral video, and 6 creators paid $80 each for a workshop on building AI idols.

---

### 5.2 Competitors - What do you understand that they don't?

**핵심 인사이트: Character.AI = 소비자 도구, 우리 = 크리에이터 플랫폼**

| | Character.AI | AIoIA |
|---|---|---|
| **모델** | B2C (소비자가 사용) | **B2B2C (크리에이터가 만들고 팬이 소비)** |
| **캐릭터** | 아무나 만든 일회성 봇 | 크리에이터가 프로듀싱한 IP |
| **관계** | 일시적 역할극 | 지속적 팬덤 |
| **수익화** | 구독 하나 | 구독 + 굿즈 + 이벤트 + 브랜드딜 |
| **비유** | 채팅 앱 | **YouTube** |

**What Character.AI doesn't understand:**
No one becomes a "fan" of a Character.AI bot. There's no community, no merchandise, no events. Why? Because there's no *creator* behind it building a narrative and relationship.

**Our insight:**
Fandom requires a creator who invests in the character. When a creator builds an AI idol with backstory, personality, and content—fans form real emotional bonds. They join communities, buy merch, attend events. This is the K-pop model, and it works: Bubble has 2.3M paying subscribers.

We're not building a better chatbot. We're building the platform that lets anyone become an AI idol producer.

### 5.3 How will you make money?

**3가지 수익원 (YouTube/Twitch 모델 + K-pop 팬덤 경제)**

| 수익원 | 크리에이터 | 플랫폼 | 벤치마크 |
|--------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **1. 구독** ($5/월) | 70% | 30% | Bubble 2.3M subs, Twitch |
| **2. 디지털 굿즈/팁** | 70% | 30% | TikTok gifts, Super Chat |
| **3. 브랜드딜** | 협의 | 수수료 | YouTube sponsorships |

**TAM 재계산 (크리에이터 플랫폼 관점):**

- AI 동반자 시장: Character.AI 20M MAU, 평균 세션 2시간
- 버튜버 시장: $3B+ (2025)
- K-pop 팬덤 경제: $12B

**보수적 추정:**
- 10,000 크리에이터 × 평균 1,000 팬 × $60/년 = **$600M GMV**
- 플랫폼 수수료 30% = **$180M 매출**
